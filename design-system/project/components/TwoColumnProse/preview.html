Run-in head

Running text sets in two columns of 233.64pt with the 20pt gap. At body size the full 487pt measure runs to about 103 characters a line, well past the 45 to 75 that read easily; a column runs to about 51.

Section titles, tables, figures and callouts keep the full measure. One measure holds for the whole document: the 54pt margin and 487.28pt, never mixed with the 36pt margin of a one-pager.

  • Bullets are 3pt navy dots, hung 10pt.
  • A list keeps the body size and leading.

A second run-in head

Body is 9 on 14 in Poppins Regular, emphasis in SemiBold. Navy carries the structure: the section badge, the subsection number, the bullets. Teal marks what already exists, amber what is conditional.

Nothing sits within 14pt of a page edge, because an office printer cannot print there. The 3pt gradient bar across the top is the single exception, and the only place the gradient appears.
